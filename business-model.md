# Vibe Rise Business Model

## The Four-Layer Stack

Four business layers, each feeding the next. Together they create a flywheel no competitor can replicate because nobody else has all four.

```
LAYER 1: ACQUISITION — /7-day-challenge (Nike Run Club for personal growth)
  Cheap consumer app. Builds massive user base. Drives brand loyalty + creator pipeline.

LAYER 2: PLATFORM — /create (GoHighLevel for experience creators)
  $97/mo SaaS. CRM + content tools + event management + AI co-founder.

LAYER 3: CERTIFICATION — Shift Architecture Training (9D Breathwork model, but broader)
  $3-5K certification + ongoing subscription. The core revenue engine.

LAYER 4: INFRASTRUCTURE — buysilentdiscoheadphones.com
  Headsets for guided experiences. Every certified facilitator must buy. Recurring hardware revenue.
```

Each layer feeds the next:
- Layer 1 users graduate to Layer 2
- Layer 2 creators graduate to Layer 3
- Layer 3 facilitators must purchase from Layer 4
- Layer 3 facilitators run events that drive Layer 1 users

## Layer 1: Acquisition — /7-day-challenge

**Comparable**: Nike Run Club

Nike Run Club has 100M+ downloads, 50M active users, generates $0 directly, but drives 73% brand loyalty and billions in attributed shoe sales. The app is a cost center that powers the real revenue.

/7-day-challenge is similar: a cheap, gamified daily practice app that builds a massive user base and feeds the creator + certification pipeline. It's not the main revenue driver. It's the acquisition funnel.

**Model**: 14-day free trial → $14.99/mo or $99/year. No credit card upfront. After trial: subscribe or drop to basic tier (daily check-in only).

**Pricing**:

|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $0 for 14 days | $0 (forever) | **$14.99/mo** or **$99/year** |

**Why 14-day trial**: Trial conversion 15-25% vs freemium 2-8%. The 14 days covers the 7-day challenge + 7 days to build the habit. The critical anti-crash window (days 1-5 after first event) needs the FULL app. No credit card upfront removes friction at QR code scan.

**Why basic tier after trial** (not full lockout): They stay in the ecosystem (push notifications, daily check-in nudge to upgrade). They don't delete the app. They can still refer friends. They're a warm lead for win-back.

**Role in the stack**: Not the revenue engine. The acquisition funnel. Covers its own costs and feeds Layers 2, 3, and 4.

**9D's estimated revenue**:

Ours are creators.
- **Consumer app as acquisition funnel**: 9D has no top-of-funnel. Their facilitators find their own clients. Our facilitators' events feed directly into FindMyFlow, which feeds back into the certification pipeline.
- **The flywheel**: 9D is linear (certify facilitators → they run events). We're circular (certify facilitators → they run events → events create app users → app users become facilitators).

- Modality-specific training: Vibe Rise Dance Journey, Healing Compass, Breathwork, Shaking
- Business launch support (via /create portal, included)
- Facilitator kit: branded materials, curated playlists, event templates
- Right to use the Vibe Rise brand for events
- Ongoing access to journey library + new modality releases
- Community of certified facilitators

**Role in the stack**: Physical infrastructure. Recurring revenue. Moat (software competitors can't replicate logistics + inventory + supplier relationships). And a lead generation channel for certification.

## How the Four Layers Work Together

```
LAYER 1: Consumer discovers Vibe Rise
  → Attends a free event OR finds the app via content
  → 14-day trial → $14.99/mo subscriber
  → Uses the app daily. Builds capacity. Feels different.
  → 3-6 months in: "I want to share this with others."

LAYER 2: Consumer becomes Creator
  → Upgrades to /create ($97/mo)
  → Uses CRM, content tools, event management
  → Starts running small events using basic templates
  → Realizes they need deeper methodology training

LAYER 3: Creator becomes Certified Facilitator
  → Enrolls in Shift Architecture certification ($3-5K)
  → Learns the science (memory reconsolidation, polyvagal, somatic discharge)
  → Gets trained in 4+ modalities (dance, shaking, breathwork, healing compass)
  → Graduates. Now a certified Vibe Rise facilitator.

LAYER 4: Facilitator buys infrastructure
  → Purchases headset fleet ($1-3K) from buysilentdiscoheadphones.com
  → Runs professional events using Shift Architecture methodology
  → Events create new Layer 1 users (attendees scan QR → FindMyFlow trial)
  → Flywheel spins. Their success IS our growth.
```

## What Makes This Investable

### 1. Proven Model (9D), Broader Application

9D Breathwork built $5-9M/year from ONE modality with NO consumer app. We have four modalities, a consumer app as the acquisition funnel, a SaaS platform, and the headset supply chain. If we reach just 40% of 9D's facilitator count (500 vs 1,300), we're at $2M+ ARR.

### 2. Near-Zero CAC Across All Layers

Events are free. Content is organic. Creators come from the consumer tier. Facilitators come from the creator tier. Headset buyers come from certification. The entire acquisition engine runs without paid ads. The only layer that benefits from ad spend is Layer 4 (headset e-commerce) which has direct ROI.

### 3. Four-Layer Moat

Anyone can build a meditation app (Layer 1). Anyone can build a creator SaaS (Layer 2). Anyone can run a certification (Layer 3). Anyone can sell headphones (Layer 4). Nobody has all four working as an integrated flywheel. The combination is the moat.

### 4. Physical + Digital = Defensible

Headsets are a physical product with logistics, inventory, and supplier relationships. Software competitors can't replicate this. The combination of SaaS + physical product + live events + certification + creator network is a multi-layered moat.

### 5. Market Timing

- Sober curious: $30B trend, Gen Z drinking down 20%
- Creator economy: $250B+ market
- Wellness apps: $6B+ market
- Experience economy: $12B+ and growing post-COVID
- Breathwork market: $1.2B → $4.8B by 2033 (16.7% CAGR)
- Dawnbreak proved consumer demand (0 → 82K in 18 months) with NO back-end

### 6. Revenue Diversification

By Year 3, four independent revenue streams. No single stream is more than 35%. App revenue covers infrastructure costs. Certification drives margin. Headsets provide recurring physical revenue. Premium adds high-ticket upside. This is not a one-trick business.
